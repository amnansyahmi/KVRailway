KV Railway is an independent, offline-ready route planner for Klang Valley, with ETS corridor exploration and planned ECRL previews across Malaysia. The network layout is based on the map designed by @ziqtofu, with service availability checked against operator sources on 14 October 2026.

A route, not a timetable

Page zoom is locked; pinch and zoom controls work inside the map only. Journey times are estimates of time on trains and transfers. They exclude waiting, delays and operating hours. The moving train is a route preview, not a live vehicle. Check your operator before travelling, especially for KTM connections.

What’s included

12 available lines, including Shah Alam Line. Tap a station for parking, facilities and connecting walks. Planned stations and MRT3 Circle Line are reference only. Skypark Link is suspended. Walking connections may require leaving ticket gates; ERL, KTM and BRT can need separate tickets.

Across Malaysia

The Malaysia view uses a bundled Natural Earth map with approximate station areas and illustrative corridors. ETS station names follow the timetable effective 1 June 2026; actual stops depend on the train. ECRL is a future preview: targeted passenger operation is January 2027 for Kota Bharu–Gombak and January 2028 for Gombak–Port Klang. No planned ECRL service is included in operating routing.

Keep it on your phone

On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: use Install app or your browser’s install menu. After the first complete load, the map and planner work offline. Saved journeys stay on this device.
